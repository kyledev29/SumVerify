Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

{{@name}} is built so that your files stay with you. This page explains what the site does and does not do with your data.

Your files

Checksums are calculated entirely in your browser. The file you choose is read locally in small pieces and is never uploaded to our servers or to anyone else. We cannot see its name, contents or size.

Hashes you paste or upload

Hashes and checksum files you enter are compared with your file in the browser and are not sent anywhere.

Known-file lookup (coming soon)

If this optional feature is released, only the calculated hash, never the file, would be sent to our database to check whether the file is publicly known. This policy will be updated before the feature goes live.

Data stored on your device

The site stores one preference in your browser’s local storage: your chosen light or dark theme (sv-theme). It is never sent to us and you can clear it at any time. We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

Third-party services

The site loads the Figtree typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser requests it from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and browser information under Google’s privacy policy. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, requested page and time) for security and operations.
